# Penne alla Vodka  

*Yield: 4 servings*  
**Ingredients:**  
* 16 ounces penne  
* 3 tablespoons olive oil  
* 1 large shallot, finely diced  
* 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped  
* A little under 1/2 cup vodka  
* 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes  
* 1 cup heavy cream  
* 4 ounces grated Parmesan, plus more for topping  
* Salt and pepper  
* Crushed red pepper flakes  
* Fresh basil for garnish  
**Instructions:**  
1. **Boil the Water:**  
    * Bring a pot of salted water to a boil.  
2. **Start the Sauce:**  
    * Heat the olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the shallots and cook for 3-5 minutes.  
    * Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds longer, until fragrant.  
    * Add the vodka, increase the heat to medium-high, and reduce by half, about 2-3 minutes.  
3. **Simmer the Tomatoes:**  
    * Add the crushed tomatoes, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Simmer for about 10 minutes, while your pasta cooks.  
4. **Cook the Pasta:**  
    * While the sauce simmers, add the pasta to the boiling water and cook until al dente.  
    * Drain, saving a little pasta water for later.  
5. **Finish the Sauce:**  
    * Once the sauce has simmered, add the heavy cream, Parmesan, red pepper flakes, and salt and pepper to taste.  
    * Add the pasta, and a splash of pasta water if needed to thin the sauce a bit.  
6. **Serve:**  
    * Top with basil, crushed red pepper flakes, and additional Parmesan if you want.
